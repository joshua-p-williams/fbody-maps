# Safety Notes

## Driver Visibility

Do not mount accessories where they obstruct the driver's forward view or create a distraction.

## Crash Safety

Interior-mounted objects can become hazards in a crash. Keep parts low-profile, rounded, and securely attached.

## Heat

Vehicle interiors can become extremely hot. PLA may deform in parked vehicles. Use appropriate material for final parts.

## Airbags

Verify vehicle airbag locations before installing anything on or near pillar trim. Do not interfere with airbags or safety restraints.

## Wiring

Route wires so they do not interfere with steering, pedals, airbags, weather stripping, or trim clips.
